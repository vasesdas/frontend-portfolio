FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Flowbase is built for everyone. The visual builder and AI assistant let you create automations without any technical knowledge.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel your plan whenever you like from your account settings.

What happens when my trial ends?

You'll be moved to the free Starter plan automatically. Your flows are saved and you can upgrade any time to reactivate them.

Is my data secure?

Security is our top priority. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, all data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never sell your data.

Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits?

Yes — eligible startups and non-profits get 50% off any paid plan for the first year. Contact our team to apply.
