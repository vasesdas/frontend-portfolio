Our story

A little piece of Italy around the corner

Casa Olivo was opened by Marco and Giulia Bianchi with one simple idea: cook the food they grew up with in Tuscany, using honest ingredients and recipes passed down through three generations.

Twenty-five years later, we still roll our pasta by hand, bake our bread every morning and greet every guest like family.

— Marco & Giulia
