About

A studio built on quiet details.

Founded in 2012 by architect Elena Novak, NOVA started as a two-person workshop in a converted Milan warehouse. Today our team of designers, architects and visualisers works with private clients and brands across Europe.

We believe a great interior is not decorated — it is composed. Every material, line and light source is chosen with intention.
